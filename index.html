Why this app exists

Solving daily student confusion: Where should I eat today?

Many students lose time every day trying to decide between MSC and The Zone. Menus are long, spread across sources, and difficult to compare quickly by meal, food type, or nutrition. This app was built to remove that friction and turn dining decisions into a simple, confident flow.

The real problem students face

On busy class days, students often have only a few minutes to decide where to eat. Without a clear comparison, students may walk to one location, dislike the options, then lose more time switching locations. For students managing goals like high-protein eating, no beef/no pork preferences, or calorie control, this confusion becomes even harder because nutrition context is usually not easy to scan in one place.

The result is common: delayed meals, rushed choices, lower satisfaction, and inconsistent progress for health goals. Students need one reliable screen that answers, quickly: what is available, which venue is better for my needs, and what should I pick now?

How this app solves it

  • Shows MSC and The Zone side-by-side for faster comparison.
  • Supports meal-focused views so users can jump to breakfast, lunch, or dinner instantly.
  • Offers filters like high protein, healthy, vegetarian, non-veg, beef, no beef, pork, and no pork.
  • Highlights food attributes and nutrition details where available.

Favorite and select functionality

  • Students can favorite preferred items to build a personal shortlist.
  • Today favorites appear with context so users can act quickly.
  • Filter controls let users effectively select exactly what they want to see.
  • Copy menu text exports filtered results for planning, sharing, or AI analysis.

Health and routine support

  • Each menu item can be added directly to the calorie tracker with one click.
  • Daily totals and trend graphs help students monitor eating consistency.
  • Saved data in local storage keeps personal history available across sessions.
  • The app is mobile installable, making it practical for on-campus use.

Student impact

This app reduces decision fatigue and saves time between classes by turning menu browsing into a guided workflow: compare options, apply your preferences, pick confidently, and track what you ate. It is designed for real student routines where speed, clarity, and healthy consistency matter every day.
